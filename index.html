06 / GOOD TO KNOWCOMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
save.

Is Social Save free to use? ＋

This interface is designed around a free, no-sign-up workflow. Hosting and third-party processing costs may apply to the service operator.

Can I download any link? ＋

No. Only supported, publicly accessible content may work. Private, restricted, deleted, DRM-protected or unsupported media may not be available.

Will a downloader backend work automatically? ＋

No. This repository's front end needs a compatible API endpoint to fetch media information and return safe download URLs. The interface will show a clear message until one is configured.

Can I reuse downloaded content? ＋

You are responsible for checking copyright, licences, creator permissions and the source platform's rules before downloading or reusing media.
