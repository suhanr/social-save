Instagram Video Downloader

Found an Instagram video worth keeping? Social Save is built around one simple workflow: copy the video's link, paste it into Social Save, and choose the available download option.

How it works

  1. Open the video in the Instagram app or on instagram.com.
  2. Tap the Share (paper plane) icon and choose Copy link. On a computer, copy the address from the browser bar.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and choose the available download option.

Which Instagram links work

Instagram uses different links for different kinds of posts. A normal feed post has /p/ in its link, a Reel has /reel/, and Stories use a separate link that stops working once the story disappears after 24 hours. Social Save recognises links from instagram.com and tells you when a link is not from a supported platform.

If you want to save a Reel specifically, our Instagram Reels downloader page has steps and tips just for that format. To save the pictures from a post instead, see the photo downloader.

What can and cannot be saved

  • Can usually be saved: videos and Reels from public accounts, when the creator has not turned downloads off.
  • Cannot be saved: posts from private accounts, deleted posts, and Stories that have already expired.
  • Depends on the creator: Instagram lets creators control whether their Reels can be downloaded, so some public videos will not be available.

Tips for a smoother result

  • Copy the full link. Share links often end with extra text after a question mark; that part is harmless.
  • Open the link in a private browser window first. If you cannot see the video there, Social Save will not be able to see it either.
  • Saved quality depends on what the creator uploaded, not on the downloader.

Who uses an Instagram video downloader

  • Creators keep public videos as visual references for their own shoots.
  • Students and researchers save educational videos to watch offline, where permitted.
  • Marketers keep examples of formats and trends for campaign reviews.
  • Everyday users save a recipe, a travel tip or a workout to watch without a connection.

Troubleshooting checklist

  1. Open the link in a private browser window. If you cannot see the video, the account is probably private.
  2. Check that the link points to a video, not to a profile or a hashtag.
  3. Copy the link again from the Share menu in case the first copy was cut off.
  4. Try a different post from the same account. If other posts work, the creator may have restricted that one.
  5. Wait a few minutes and try again. Availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

Photos and carousels

Instagram posts can hold several pictures or a mix of photos and video. For pictures, use the photo downloader; the workflow is the same, and it also applies to the first image of a carousel link.

Save responsibly

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Frequently asked questions

Is the Social Save Instagram video downloader free?

Yes. Social Save is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark added by Social Save.

Can I download videos from a private Instagram account?

No. Social Save only works with publicly accessible links. If an account is private, only approved followers can see its posts, and Social Save cannot access them.

Why is my Instagram link not working?

Check that the post is public and still exists, that you copied the whole link, and that it really comes from Instagram. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

Where does a saved video go on my phone?

It depends on your device and browser. Most phones place downloads in the Downloads folder or the gallery. See our guide to where downloaded videos go on Android.
