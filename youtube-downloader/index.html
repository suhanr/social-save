How it works

  1. Copy a supported URL from the platform.
  2. Paste it into Social Save.
  3. Choose the available download option.

Links this page understands

  • Regular videos: youtube.com/watch?v=…
  • Short links from the Share menu: youtu.be/…
  • Shorts: youtube.com/shorts/… (see the Shorts downloader)

YouTube's own options come first

YouTube's terms generally allow downloading only through features YouTube provides. YouTube Premium offers offline downloads inside the YouTube app, and creators can download their own uploads from YouTube Studio. If one of those covers what you need, use it.

When a downloader is appropriate

Use a downloader only for content you own, content with a licence that permits it, or where you have the creator's permission. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Our plain-language guide, Is it legal to download social media videos?, explains the basics. It is general information, not legal advice.

If your YouTube link will not work

  1. Check that the video is public and not age-restricted or removed.
  2. Make sure the link points to a single video, not a channel or playlist.
  3. Copy it again from the Share menu.
