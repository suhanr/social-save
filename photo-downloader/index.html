How it works

  1. Open the post that contains the photo and copy its link from the Share menu.
  2. Paste the link into the box above.
  3. Choose the available download option for the photo.

Photos on social media

Photos are shared as single images and as carousels that hold several pictures in one post. Quality depends on what the owner uploaded and on which sizes the platform makes available; a photo that was small to start with cannot be made sharper by a downloader.

Common photo formats

FormatGood forNotes
JPGPhotographsSmall files, widely supported, slight quality loss.
PNGGraphics, screenshots, textLarger files, supports transparency.
WebPWeb imagesSmaller than JPG at similar quality, newer.

People and privacy

A photo of a person is also about that person. Keep saved photos for personal use and do not share or publish pictures of other people without their permission. For images you can reuse freely, browse licensed libraries through our free stock media page instead.

Getting the best quality

  • Choose the largest version offered; thumbnails are much smaller than the original.
  • Do not edit and re-save a JPG repeatedly: each save loses a little quality.
  • Keep the original file and make edits on a copy.

Common uses

  • Keeping a photo you were tagged in, after asking the person who posted it.
  • Saving a product picture or a recipe photo for personal reference.
  • Collecting images for a mood board, with credit and permission if you plan to publish.

If a photo will not save

  1. Check that the post is public and still available.
  2. Make sure you pasted the post link, not the image file address from another site.
  3. Try a different post from the same account to see whether the restriction is for one post only.

Save responsibly

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Frequently asked questions

Can I download photos from Instagram and Facebook?

Photos from public posts can be supported, as long as the owner has not restricted them. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

What file format will I get?

That depends on the source. Most social photos are JPG or WebP.

Can I use a downloaded photo on my website?

Only with the owner's permission or a licence that allows it. For free-to-use images, see our guide to free stock sites.

Does Social Save add a watermark to photos?

No. Social Save does not add its own watermark.

Why can't I save a carousel?

Availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.
