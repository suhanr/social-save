YouTube Shorts Downloader

Copy the YouTube Short's link, paste it into Social Save, and choose the available download option. Read the notes below about YouTube's terms first.

Works with
  • YouTube

How it works

  1. Open the Short in the YouTube app and tap Share.
  2. Choose Copy link. Shorts links contain /shorts/.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and choose the available download option.

Shorts links

A Short opens at an address that looks like youtube.com/shorts/…. Sharing from the app can also give you a short youtu.be/… link. Social Save recognises both forms as YouTube links. For regular long videos, use the YouTube video downloader page.

YouTube's own options

YouTube's terms generally allow downloading only through features YouTube provides. Two official routes are worth knowing: Save adds a Short to a playlist such as Watch later, and YouTube Premium includes offline downloads inside the YouTube app. If you are the creator, YouTube Studio lets you download your own uploads.

Before you use a downloader

Check YouTube's terms and the creator's rights. Saving a Short for private viewing is different from reuploading it. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

If a Shorts link will not work

  1. Check that the Short is public and has not been removed.
  2. Copy the link again from the Share menu.
  3. Open it in a private window; if it plays there, the link is fine.
  4. Remember that availability can vary depending on the source and that creators control their content.

Save responsibly

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. How do I copy a YouTube Shorts link?

Open the Short, tap Share and choose Copy link. The link contains /shorts/ or is a short youtu.be link.

Can I download a Short from my own channel?

Yes. As the creator you can download your own uploads from YouTube Studio.

Does YouTube allow downloading Shorts?

YouTube's terms generally permit downloads only through features YouTube provides, such as offline downloads with YouTube Premium. Read the terms and respect the creator's rights.

Why won't my Shorts link work?

Check that the Short is public and still available, and that you copied the full link. Social Save works with publicly accessible links.
