From social media videos and Reels to Shorts, photos and free stock media, Social Save brings useful downloading tools together in one place.

Our goal is simple: make saving online media easier, faster and more accessible without unnecessary complexity.

One Place for Your Media

The internet is full of content worth keeping. A useful video, an inspiring Reel, a memorable photo or a piece of stock footage can quickly disappear into an endless social feed.

Social Save is designed to make that process simpler.

Our platform provides easy-to-use tools for downloading supported media from popular platforms and accessing free stock media from the web.

Whether you are a content creator, student, marketer, researcher or everyday internet user, Social Save helps you save the content you need with fewer steps.

Social Media Downloader

Social Save provides a collection of social media downloading tools designed around common media formats and platforms.

Depending on platform support, you can use Social Save to download:

  • Social media videos
  • Instagram Reels
  • Facebook videos and Reels
  • TikTok videos
  • YouTube videos and Shorts
  • Photos and other supported media

The service is designed around a simple workflow: copy a supported URL, paste it into Social Save, and choose the available download option.

Built for Simplicity

We believe a good downloader should not feel complicated.

That is why Social Save focuses on a clean interface, straightforward controls and fast access to the tools people actually need.

There are no complicated settings to learn. You paste a link, process it and download the available media.

More Than a Video Downloader

Social Save is also expanding beyond traditional social media downloading.

Our free stock media tools are designed to help users discover and download supported stock photos and stock videos for creative and personal projects.

This makes Social Save useful not only for downloading social media content, but also for finding practical media resources in one place.

Social Save Android App

Social Save is also available as an Android app, bringing our media tools directly to mobile devices.

The app is designed for users who frequently work with online media and want access to downloading tools from their phone.

The Android experience focuses on convenience, speed and a simple workflow.

For Creators and Everyday Users

Social Save can be useful for many different types of users.

Content creators can use media tools to organize and save references. Social media users can keep useful videos and photos for personal use. Students and researchers can save publicly available media for reference, where permitted. Marketers and digital professionals can use supported stock media resources for creative workflows.

Our goal is to build tools that are useful without making the experience unnecessarily complicated.

Our Approach

Social Save is built around three simple principles:

  • SimpleThe tools should be easy to understand and use.
  • FastThe path from URL to available download should be as straightforward as possible.
  • UsefulEvery feature should solve a real media-saving problem.

Use Media Responsibly

Social Save provides technical tools for downloading supported online media. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with the copyright, licensing terms, platform rules and applicable laws.

You should only download or reuse content when you have the necessary permission or when the content is otherwise legally available for your intended use.

Keep Exploring

Social Save is continuously evolving with new tools, better experiences and additional media resources.

Explore our social media downloader tools, free stock media resources and Android app to find the option that works best for you.
