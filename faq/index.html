Frequently asked questions

Answers about Social Save, how to use the downloader tools, why a link might not work, and the rights and privacy basics.

About Social Save

What is Social Save?

Social Save is a simple media downloading platform that brings tools for saving supported videos, Reels, Shorts, photos and free stock media together in one place. It is available on the web and as an Android app.

Is Social Save free?

Yes. Social Save is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark added by Social Save.

Which platforms does Social Save work with?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, including Reels and Shorts, plus supported photos. Platform support can change over time.

Can I save videos in my browser on this website?

This website checks your link and identifies the platform, but online downloads are not switched on yet. The Social Save Android app brings the tools to your phone.

Using the tools

How do I use Social Save?

Copy a supported URL, paste it into Social Save and choose the available download option. See how to copy a video link for each platform.

Why is my link not working?

Check that the post is public and still available, that you copied the whole link and that it comes from a supported platform. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, and creators can switch off downloads. Availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

What quality will I get?

It depends on what the creator uploaded and on which versions the platform makes available. A downloader cannot add detail that was not in the original.

Which file format will I get?

Most videos are MP4 and most photos are JPG or WebP. See MP4 vs WebM vs MOV for how to choose.

Where do downloaded files go?

That depends on your device and browser. Most phones use the Downloads folder or the gallery. See where downloaded videos go on Android.

Privacy, rights and legal

Can Social Save download from private accounts?

No. Social Save works with publicly accessible links only.

Is it legal to download social media videos?

It depends on the content, the platform's terms and the laws where you live. Read our plain-language guide. It is general information, not legal advice.

What happens to the links I paste?

Our Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, including URLs and media requests, and how it is used.

How do I report a problem or a copyright concern?

Use the Contact page. Include the page or tool you were using and enough detail for us to understand and review the issue.

Use media responsibly

Social Save provides technical tools for downloading supported online media. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with the copyright, licensing terms, platform rules and applicable laws.
