Instagram Reels Downloader

Reels are short vertical videos, and they are easy to lose in a feed. Copy the Reel's link, paste it into Social Save, and choose the available download option.

Paste a link

Works with
  • Instagram

How it works

  1. Open the Reel and tap the Share (paper plane) icon.
  2. Choose Copy link. The link contains /reel/.
  3. Paste it into the box above and choose the available download option.

Why people save Reels

Creators keep Reels as visual references, marketers save examples of formats that work, and everyday users want a recipe, workout or tutorial available without a connection. Instagram's own bookmark saves a Reel inside the app only; it does not put a file on your phone. A link-based downloader is designed for the second case.

Reels and music

Many Reels use licensed music or audio from other creators. A saved Reel normally keeps that audio, but the right to use it often covers Instagram only. Watching offline is one thing; posting the same clip on another platform can trigger a copyright claim.

Why some Reels cannot be saved

  • The creator has turned off downloads for their Reels.
  • The account is private or the Reel has been removed.
  • The link is incomplete, or it is a Story or profile link instead of a Reel link.

Troubleshooting a Reel link

  1. Check that the link contains /reel/. If it contains /p/, use the Instagram video downloader instead.
  2. Make sure the account is public. Private accounts are never accessible.
  3. If the Reel was shared to you in a message, open it first and copy the link from the Share menu.
  4. A very new or just-edited Reel can take a moment to become available. Try again later.

Ideas for using saved Reels

  • Keep a private folder of recipes, workouts and tutorials you want to follow offline.
  • Collect examples of editing styles you like, for your own inspiration.
  • Save a Reel you created, so you have the original file and not only the app version.

Save responsibly

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Frequently asked questions

How do I copy a Reel link?

Open the Reel, tap the paper plane Share icon and choose Copy link. On a computer, copy the address from the browser bar. The link will contain /reel/.

Will a saved Reel include its music?

Usually the audio is part of the video. The licence for that music often covers Instagram only, so check before reposting anywhere else.

Does Social Save add a watermark to Reels?

No. Social Save does not add its own watermark. Any logo or name that is already part of the original video stays as the creator published it.

Why can I save one Reel but not another?

Creators can switch off downloads for their Reels, and private or removed posts are not available. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.
