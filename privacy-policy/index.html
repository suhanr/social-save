At Social Save, we respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our website, services and Android application handle information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Social Save, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using Social Save, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Social Save

Social Save is a media utility platform that provides tools for downloading supported videos, Reels, Shorts, photos and other online media. Social Save is available through our website and Android application.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Social Save website
  • Social Save web-based downloader tools
  • The Social Save Android application
  • Contact and support interactions with Social Save

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is necessary to operate and improve Social Save.

Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you contact us, submit feedback, report a problem or request support.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • The content of your message
  • Information you choose to provide about a technical issue

We do not require you to create an account simply to use the website’s basic downloading tools unless a particular feature specifically requires one.

URLs and Media Requests

When you use a Social Save downloader, you may submit a URL from a supported platform.

Depending on how the requested tool operates, the submitted URL may be processed by our servers in order to retrieve information about the requested media and provide the available download functionality.

Please do not submit private, confidential or sensitive information through a downloader URL.

Technical Information

Like most websites and online services, Social Save may receive limited technical information when you access our services. This can include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring pages
  • Basic usage information
  • Date and time of requests

This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring and service improvement.

3. How We Use Information

Information we receive may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Social Save services
  • Process supported media requests
  • Respond to support enquiries
  • Investigate technical problems
  • Detect abuse, fraud or security threats
  • Improve website and application performance
  • Understand general usage patterns
  • Maintain and improve our products and features
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use information collected through our services for purposes unrelated to operating, securing or improving Social Save unless permitted or required by law.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Social Save may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Maintaining website functionality
  • Remembering preferences
  • Understanding website usage
  • Improving performance
  • Security and fraud prevention

Third-party services used on our website may also place their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective privacy policies.

You can control or disable certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to help us understand website traffic, monitor performance, provide hosting or infrastructure, prevent abuse and improve our services.

Depending on the services currently implemented on the website or application, these providers may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We do not claim responsibility for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services that are outside our control.

6. Advertising

Social Save may use advertising services to support the operation of the website or application.

Advertising providers may use cookies, identifiers or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertising in accordance with their own policies and applicable requirements.

Where applicable, you should review the privacy policies and settings provided by the relevant advertising platform.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations and enforcing our policies.

The retention period may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Social Save against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, application, server or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should therefore avoid submitting passwords, financial information or other highly sensitive information through ordinary support forms or downloader requests.

9. Children’s Privacy

Social Save is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Links

Social Save may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services.

These websites operate independently from Social Save and may have their own privacy policies, terms and data practices.

We encourage users to review the privacy policy of any third-party service they visit.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to certain personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us using the information provided on our Contact page.

We may need to verify your request before taking action.

12. Copyright and User Responsibility

Social Save provides technical tools for accessing and downloading supported online media.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of downloaded content complies with applicable copyright laws, licensing requirements, intellectual property rights and the terms of the relevant platform.

Social Save does not encourage users to download or redistribute content they do not have permission to use.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how Social Save handles information.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices or a privacy-related request, please contact us through our Contact page.
