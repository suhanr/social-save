Contact

Have a question about Social Save, our downloading tools, or the Android app? We would be happy to hear from you.

Whether you are experiencing a technical issue, want to report a problem, have a suggestion, or simply need help using one of our tools, you can get in touch with the Social Save team.

Get in Touch

We welcome questions, feedback and reports from our users.

For the fastest support, please include as much relevant information as possible when contacting us. For technical issues, mentioning the page or tool you were using and briefly describing the problem can help us understand the issue more quickly.

Technical Support

Having trouble with a Social Save downloader or another feature?

Tell us what happened, which tool you were using, and, where relevant, the URL or error message you encountered. We will use the information to investigate the issue.

Feedback & Suggestions

Social Save is continuously being improved, and user feedback helps us decide what to build next.

Have an idea for a new downloader, a better feature, or an improvement to the website or Android app? Send it our way.

Report a Problem

Found a broken link, incorrect information, unexpected behavior or another issue on Social Save?

Please let us know so we can investigate and improve the service.

Before Contacting Us

For common questions, you may also want to explore our Social Media Downloader tools, Free Stock Media resources, Journal and About page.

When contacting us about copyright or other content-related concerns, please provide sufficient information for us to understand and review the issue.
