Facebook Video Downloader

Facebook videos can disappear from a timeline as quickly as they arrive. Copy the video's link, paste it into Social Save and choose the available download option.

How it works

  1. Open the video or Reel on Facebook and tap Share.
  2. Choose Copy link. Short links that start with fb.watch also work.
  3. Paste the link into the box above and choose the available download option.

Facebook video links explained

Facebook shares videos in several forms: regular posts, the Watch tab, Reels and live videos that have ended. Links can look like facebook.com/watch/?v=…, facebook.com/reel/… or the short fb.watch/…. Social Save recognises all of these as Facebook links.

Public and private videos

Only videos that anyone can open can be saved. A video shared with friends, inside a private group, or with limited audience settings is not available to Social Save, even if you can see it while logged in. If you want a copy of a video you uploaded yourself, Facebook's own tools are the better route. Our guide to saving Facebook videos compares the options.

Troubleshooting

  • Open the link in a private browser window. If Facebook asks you to log in, the video is not public.
  • Copy the link from the Share menu rather than from a screenshot or a message preview.
  • A live video becomes a normal video after it ends, but only if the owner leaves it public.

Who saves Facebook videos

  • Local organisations that want a copy of a public announcement for their records.
  • Families keeping a video they were tagged in, after asking the person who posted it.
  • Students keeping lecture-style videos for offline study, where allowed.

If a Facebook link will not work

  1. Open the link while logged out or in a private window. A login prompt means it is not public.
  2. Check that you copied a video link and not the link to a whole page or album.
  3. For fb.watch links, copy a fresh one from the Share menu.
  4. Ask the owner to change the audience to Public, if you are the intended viewer and they agree.

Save responsibly

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Frequently asked questions

Is the Facebook video downloader free?

Yes. Social Save is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark added by Social Save.

Can I save a video from a private group?

No. Social Save only works with publicly accessible links. Videos in private groups or shared with limited audiences cannot be accessed.

Does it work with fb.watch links?

Yes. Social Save recognises fb.watch short links as Facebook links.

Can I download Facebook Reels?

Reels from public accounts are treated like other public Facebook videos, as long as the creator allows it. Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.
