Where Do Downloaded Videos Go on Android? Find, Organise and Free Up Space

You tapped download and the video vanished. It is almost always in one of three places. Here is how to find it, organise it and clear space.

Check the Downloads folder first

  1. Open the Files app (often called Files by Google, or My Files on some phones).
  2. Tap Downloads.
  3. Sort by date to bring the newest file to the top.

Then the gallery

Many phones show saved videos and photos in Google Photos or the built-in Gallery. Look for albums called Download, Movies or Pictures. Names vary by manufacturer.

Browser downloads

In Chrome, open the three-dot menu and choose Downloads to see what you have saved and open the file straight from the list. If the file is not listed, the download may not have finished, so check your notifications for a failed download.

If you still cannot find it

  • Search by the file name or by the type, such as videos, in the Files app.
  • Check whether the download went to an SD card instead of the phone.
  • Look at the notification shade: tapping a finished download notification opens the file.
  • Check storage: a full phone can make downloads fail without a clear error.

Organise what you keep

  • Create a folder for each project or topic.
  • Rename files so you can search for them later.
  • Back up important videos to cloud storage.

Free up space

  • Sort Files by size to spot the biggest videos.
  • Delete duplicates and clips you no longer need.
  • Move older files to a computer or an SD card, if your phone supports one.

Keep in mind

Steps and names differ between phone makers and Android versions. Only keep videos you have the right to keep, and see our guide to the legal basics. The Social Save downloader tools are also available as an Android app.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my downloaded videos not in the gallery?

They may be in the Downloads folder, which some galleries do not scan. Open the Files app to find them.

How do I move downloads to an SD card?

In the Files app, select the file, choose Move and pick the SD card. Not every phone has an SD card slot.

Does deleting a video from the gallery free up space?

Yes, but check any Trash or Recently deleted folder, which can keep files for a while.
