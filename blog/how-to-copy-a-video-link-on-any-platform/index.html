How to Copy a Video Link From Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube

Every downloader, and every share message, starts with a link. Here is where to find the Copy link option on the four biggest platforms, on a phone and on a computer, and what to check before you paste it anywhere.

Instagram

  1. Open the post or Reel in the Instagram app.
  2. Tap the paper plane Share icon below the video.
  3. Choose Copy link.

Post links contain /p/ and Reel links contain /reel/. Instagram often adds extra text after a question mark, such as ?igsh=. It is a tracking tag; you can leave it in or remove it, and the link still opens the same post.

Facebook

  1. Open the video, Reel or post.
  2. Tap Share, then Copy link.

Facebook links can look long, like facebook.com/watch/?v=…, or short, like fb.watch/…. Both are valid. If you only see a Share to Feed option, scroll the Share sheet: Copy link is usually listed with the other sharing actions.

TikTok

  1. Open the video and tap the Share arrow.
  2. Choose Copy link.

TikTok gives you either a full link, tiktok.com/@name/video/…, or a short one on a tiktok.com subdomain such as vm.tiktok.com. A short link redirects to the full one when you open it.

YouTube

  1. Open the video or Short and tap Share.
  2. Choose Copy link.

You will get youtu.be/…. Regular videos also have youtube.com/watch?v=… and Shorts use youtube.com/shorts/…. All three point to the same kind of content, just written differently.

On a computer

Open the video in your browser and copy the address from the address bar. On most browsers you can click the bar once, press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac) and paste it wherever you need it. If the page address does not show the full link, the Share button on the page will give you a clean version.

Domaininstagram.comTells you which platform the link belongs to
Path/reel/…Says what kind of post it is: a Reel, a post, a Short

Common mistakes

  • Copying the profile link. A link to an account is not a link to a video. Open the specific video first.
  • Copying from a screenshot or a message preview. Previews can show shortened text. Copy from the Share menu.
  • Using a link from a private place. Links from private groups or private accounts only open for people with access.
  • A link that stops halfway. Long links sometimes get cut when pasted into chat apps. Paste it in a notes app first to check it is complete.

Quick check before you paste

  • Is the post public? Open the link in a private window; if it asks you to log in, it is not public.
  • Did you copy the whole link?
  • Is it from the right platform? Social Save tells you when it is not.

Once you have the link, paste it into Social Save, or use the page for your platform: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or YouTube.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to remove the extra text after the question mark?

No. Text such as ?igsh= is a tracking tag. The link works with or without it.

Why does my copied link open the app instead of a webpage?

On a phone, links to these platforms often open the installed app. To check a link in a browser, paste it into a private browser window.

Can I copy a link to a Story?

You can, but Stories expire after 24 hours, so the link stops working once the story has gone.
