How to Save Facebook Videos: Official and Link-Based Options

Facebook gives you a few official ways to keep videos, and a link-based downloader covers some of the gaps. Here is how they compare, and what to do when a video will not save.

Option 1: Save for later (inside Facebook)

Tap the three-dot menu on a post and choose Save video. It goes to your Saved list, which you can open from the menu on the app or on the website. It is a bookmark, not a file, so it can vanish if the owner deletes the video or changes who can see it.

Option 2: Download your own videos

If you uploaded the video, Facebook's own tools let you download a copy of your information, including the videos you posted. You choose what to include and the format, then Facebook prepares a file for you to download. This is the most reliable way to keep your own content, and it needs no third-party tool.

Option 3: Use a link-based downloader for public videos

  1. Open the video and tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into Social Save.
  3. Choose the available download option.

This only works with videos that anyone can open. Videos shared with friends, inside private groups, or with limited audience settings cannot be accessed.

Fixes for common problems

  • The link asks for a login. The video is not public.
  • A fb.watch link does not work. Copy the link again from the Share menu; short links can expire.
  • A live video is missing. A live stream becomes a normal video after it ends only if the owner leaves it public.
  • The video plays but nothing is offered. The owner may have restricted it. Availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

Respect people and rights

Facebook videos often show other people, including children. Do not share or publish a video of someone without their permission, and treat the creator's copyright seriously. See our guide to the legal basics.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download a Facebook video someone sent me in Messenger?

That depends on how it was shared. If you have the original public link, it can work; a video shared privately in a chat is not public.

Is it free to save Facebook videos with Social Save?

Social Save is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark added by Social Save.

Why can I watch a video but not save it?

Being able to watch a video while logged in does not make it public. Social Save only works with videos anyone can open.

Does Facebook tell people when I save their video?

Saving a video inside Facebook is private. It does not notify the owner.
