MP4 vs WebM vs MOV: Which Video Format Should You Save?

When a download tool offers several formats, MP4 is almost always the safe pick. Here is why, when the others make sense, and what to know about image formats too.

Container versus codec

A video file has two parts: a container (the file type, such as MP4) and a codec (how the picture is compressed). Two MP4 files can behave differently if they use different codecs, but most MP4 files use widely supported ones.

MP4

The most compatible choice. It plays on phones, computers, TVs and in every major editor, and it is what most social platforms deliver. If you are unsure, choose MP4.

WebM

Designed for the web, WebM often gives smaller files. Support has improved, but it is still less universal than MP4, especially in older devices and some editors.

MOV

Apple's container, common from iPhones and Mac editing. It works well in Apple software, tends to produce larger files, and is sometimes awkward on other systems.

Resolution, frame rate and bitrate

  • Resolution is the number of pixels, such as 720p or 1080p. More pixels means a sharper picture and a bigger file.
  • Frame rate is how many pictures appear per second. 30 is common for social video; 60 looks smoother.
  • Bitrate is how much data is used per second. A low bitrate makes a small file with visible blur.

Image formats

  • JPG for photographs: small, universal.
  • PNG for graphics and screenshots: sharp edges, supports transparency.
  • WebP for web images: smaller than JPG at similar quality.

Quality comes from the source

A downloader cannot add detail that was never in the upload. If the original is low resolution, any format will look the same. Choose the highest quality offered and keep the original if you plan to edit.

File size and storage

A one-minute 1080p MP4 is usually many megabytes, and long collections add up quickly. If your phone is full, see how to find and clear downloaded files on Android.

Frequently asked questions

Which format is best for editing?

MP4 works in almost every editor. If you use Apple software, MOV can also work well.

Why is my video file so big?

Higher resolution, higher frame rates and higher bitrates all increase file size.

Can I convert WebM to MP4?

Yes. Many video editors and converters can do it. Check the licence of the video before you edit and share it.
