How to Save TikTok Videos (and What the Watermark Means)

TikTok offers a built-in way to save videos, but it comes with a watermark, and creators can switch it off. Here is how the options differ and what the watermark really is.

Option 1: TikTok's own Save button

Tap Share on a video and, when the creator allows it, choose Save video. The file lands in your gallery with TikTok's logo and the creator's username on it. That overlay is part of the saved file.

Option 2: Favorites

Tap the bookmark icon to add the video to your Favorites. It stays inside TikTok, so it is not a file and may disappear if the video is removed or the account goes private.

Option 3: A link-based downloader

  1. Tap Share and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into the Social Save TikTok video downloader.
  3. Choose the available download option.

Social Save does not add a watermark of its own. It does not remove anything that is already part of the original video.

What the watermark is for

The moving TikTok logo and the username are credit. They tell viewers where a clip came from and who made it. If you plan to share a saved clip, keeping that credit is the fair thing to do, and removing someone's name from their work is a common cause of copyright and community complaints.

Why you cannot save some TikToks

  • The creator turned off downloads.
  • The account is private or the video is friends-only.
  • The video was deleted, or the link is incomplete.
  • You copied a link to a profile, a sound or a hashtag instead of a video.

Sounds and copyright

Sounds on TikTok are often licensed for use on TikTok. A saved video is fine to watch later, but posting it on another platform may cause a copyright claim, and the creator still owns the clip. Read our guide to the legal basics.

Tips if you are a creator

You control whether others can download your videos. Check your privacy settings and decide what is right for each video. If you want people to share your work, leaving downloads on keeps the watermark credit attached.

Frequently asked questions

Does Social Save remove the TikTok watermark?

No. Social Save adds no watermark of its own and does not claim to remove anything that is part of the original video.

Do TikTok short links such as vm.tiktok.com work?

Yes. Short links on tiktok.com subdomains are recognised as TikTok links.

Can I save a TikTok photo post?

Photo posts are different from video posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.
