How to Save Instagram Reels: 4 Ways Compared

There is more than one way to keep an Instagram Reel, and they do different things. Here is what each method gives you, when it fails, and how to choose.

Method 1: Save inside Instagram

Tap the bookmark icon on a Reel and it goes to your Saved collection inside the app. You can sort saves into collections, such as recipes or workouts. It is the quickest way to find a Reel again, but it does not create a video file on your phone, and the Reel disappears from your list if the creator deletes it or makes the account private.

Method 2: Download your own Reels

If the Reel is yours, open it, tap the three-dot menu and look for a save or download option. Menus change between app versions, so look for wording such as Save to device. This is the cleanest route because you own the content, and the file is as close to the original as Instagram offers.

As a creator you also decide whether other people can download your Reels. Look in the Reel's settings for the option that controls downloads and set it the way you want.

Method 3: Use a link-based downloader

  1. Open the Reel, tap Share and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste the link into a downloader such as the Social Save Instagram Reels downloader.
  3. Choose the available download option.

This only works for public Reels, and only if the creator has not switched downloads off. A link-based tool is the best fit when you want a file of someone else's public Reel for personal reference. Not sure how to find the link? See how to copy a video link on any platform.

Method 4: Screen recording

Both iPhone and Android have a built-in screen recorder. It works on almost anything you can see, even when downloads are blocked, but it records the interface too, notifications can appear in the clip, and quality is usually lower than the original. Treat it as a last resort, and remember that it does not change who owns the Reel.

Why a Reel might not be savable

  • The account is private.
  • The creator turned off downloads.
  • The Reel was deleted, or the link is incomplete or points to a profile instead of the Reel.

Keeping saved Reels organised

Rename files so you remember what each one is, put them in a folder per topic, and note where each came from so you can credit the creator later. If you are not sure where files end up on your phone, read where downloaded videos go on Android.

A note on reuse

Keeping a Reel to watch later is not the same as reposting it. Music and footage in Reels often belong to other people. Ask the creator before you reuse anything, and read our guide to the legal basics.

Frequently asked questions

Does Instagram notify people when I save their Reel?

Saving a Reel inside Instagram is private. Instagram does not tell the creator who bookmarked it.

Can I save a Reel with its original audio?

A saved Reel normally keeps its audio, but the music licence often covers Instagram only. Check before reposting anywhere else.

Why is the saved Reel lower quality than the app?

Quality depends on the version of the video that is available, not on the method. A screen recording is usually the lowest quality.
