How to Save YouTube Shorts: Official Options First

YouTube's terms generally allow downloads only through features YouTube provides, so start with the official options. Here they are, in order, with the limits of each.

1. Save to a playlist

Tap Save on a Short to add it to Watch later or one of your own playlists. It stays in YouTube, ready whenever you have a connection. It is the simplest way to keep a Short you want to find again.

2. YouTube Premium offline downloads

YouTube Premium subscribers can download videos inside the YouTube app for offline viewing. The downloaded video plays only inside the app, and it is meant for watching without a connection, not for editing or sharing.

3. Download your own Shorts

If you are the creator, open YouTube Studio, find the video in Content and use the download option for your own upload. You get the file you uploaded, not a lower-quality copy.

4. A link-based tool, where it is appropriate

Use a link-based tool only for content you own, content with a licence that permits it, or where you have the creator's permission.

  1. Tap Share on the Short and choose Copy link.
  2. Paste it into the Social Save YouTube Shorts downloader.
  3. Choose the available download option.

What you should not do

  • Reupload someone else's Short as your own.
  • Remove credit or captions.
  • Use a Short in an advert without a licence.

Frequently asked questions

Are YouTube Shorts different from regular videos for downloading?

The link format differs (/shorts/), but the same terms and creator rights apply.

Can I download a Short from my own channel?

Yes. Creators can download their own uploads from YouTube Studio.

Why does my Shorts link not work?

Check that the Short is public and still available, and that you copied the full link.
