What Social Save is designed for

Social Save is a simple, practical media downloading tool for people who want an easier way to save content from the web: content creators, students, marketers, researchers and everyday internet users. There are no complicated settings to learn. You paste a link, process it and download the available media.

Limits to know about

Social Save works with publicly accessible links. Private accounts, deleted posts and age-restricted content cannot be saved, creators can switch off downloads for their own posts, and availability can vary depending on the source platform, media type and other technical factors.

Copyright still applies to anything you save. Keep a copy for personal reference when that is allowed, ask the creator before you repost or reuse it, and follow the platform's rules. Troubleshooting

  1. Open the link in a private browser window. If it does not play, it is not public.
  2. Check that the link points to a specific video or photo, not to a profile.
  3. Copy the link again from the platform's Share menu.
  4. Try another post from the same account. If it works, the creator may have restricted the first one.
