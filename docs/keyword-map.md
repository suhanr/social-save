## Realistic expectations

Generic head terms such as "video downloader" are dominated by long-established sites with large link profiles. A new site usually earns its first traffic from specific, lower-competition queries (a platform plus a task, a question, a problem) and then grows. The guides and landing pages target exactly those. Rankings also depend on the tool working and on other sites linking to you; see `off-page-seo.md`.
